GGJ Participations and awards

Since I started participating in Game Jams in 2023, I’ve loved the feeling of creating something experimental and new over a weekend with friends. Here are some of the games I worked on that received awards.

Mucho Macho - GGJ 2026

Best Fit of the Theme Award

Asymmetric cooperative puzzle game, combining mobile and PC gameplay, connected in local network. Team up across mobile and PC in this cooperative puzzle game to track down the intruder!

Pop The Pops - GGJ 2025

Best UX Award

In this 2-player arcade experience, you compete head-to-head, racing to reach the top while throwing the ball to gain the advantage. Fast-paced and full of strategy, every throw counts as you try to outscore and outmaneuver your opponent.

Tickle War - GGJ 2024

Best Narrative Award

In this 2D platformer, you face off against filthy enemies and challenging bosses, defeating them in the most unexpected way—by tickling! Fast-paced, playful, and full of humor.
